OUR STORY

स्वाद भी, विश्वास भी

Serving authentic taste since 1990.

Shree Shyam Chanachur is a trusted and well-known local food business that began its journey in 1990 in Mango, Jamshedpur, under the guidance of Mr. Binod Kumar Gupta.

With a simple vision of providing fresh, tasty, and high-quality chanachur, the business started as a small local venture and gradually earned the trust and love of customers through its consistent taste and quality.

For more than three decades, we have continued to focus on the things that matter most — great taste, freshness, quality ingredients, and customer satisfaction.
